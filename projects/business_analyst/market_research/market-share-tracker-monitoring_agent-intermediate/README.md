# Market Share Tracker: Monitoring Agent

**Domain:** business_analyst | **Subdomain:** market_research | **Difficulty:** intermediate | **Est. build time:** 4-8 hours

---

## Overview

Market Share Tracker built as a Monitoring agent. Market share tracking divides each competitor's revenue or units by the total market each period. Share shifts reveal who is winning before revenue totals do. The tracker plots share trends and flags statistically meaningful moves versus noise. Samples synthetic topic metrics for N cycles, evaluates each reading against thresholds, prints readings and alerts.

**Core concept — Market Share Tracker:** Market share tracking divides each competitor's revenue or units by the total market each period. Share shifts reveal who is winning before revenue totals do. The tracker plots share trends and flags statistically meaningful moves versus noise.

**Why this matters in the real world:** Strategy teams track share quarterly to measure competitive momentum.

---

## Features

- **Monitor**: Samples metrics, evaluates thresholds, and collects alerts.
- `read_metrics` (`read_metrics(rng, cycle)`): Sample one synthetic topic metric reading.
- `check_thresholds` (`check_thresholds(reading, thresholds)`): Return a list of alert strings for readings over threshold.
- `Monitor.run` (`Monitor.run(self, cycles, interval)`): Sample `cycles` readings, print them, return alert count.
- `main` (`main()`): Parse args and run the monitor.

## How It Works

The project is built around Periodic sampling with static threshold evaluation. Input data flows from src/main.py through the processing modules, and the overall approach runs in O(cycles) total; O(1) per sample. See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) for the high-level design and [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md) for the detailed module breakdown.
