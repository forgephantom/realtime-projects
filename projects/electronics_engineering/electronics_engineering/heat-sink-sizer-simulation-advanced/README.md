# Heat Sink Sizer: Simulation (Logistic Growth)

**Domain:** electronics_engineering | **Subdomain:** electronics_engineering | **Difficulty:** advanced | **Est. build time:** 8-16 hours

---

## Overview

Heat Sink Sizer built as a Simulation (logistic growth). Junction temperature follows Tj = Ta + P x theta_ja, where theta_ja is the total thermal resistance from silicon to air. Sizing a heat sink means picking one whose thermal resistance keeps the junction below its rated maximum at the worst-case ambient temperature. Euler-integrates logistic growth dP/dt = r*P*(1-P/K) and prints the trajectory table with summary statistics.

**Core concept — Heat Sink Sizer:** Junction temperature follows Tj = Ta + P x theta_ja, where theta_ja is the total thermal resistance from silicon to air. Sizing a heat sink means picking one whose thermal resistance keeps the junction below its rated maximum at the worst-case ambient temperature.

**Why this matters in the real world:** Voltage regulators, power transistors, and LED drivers in enclosed products all need this thermal check.

---

## Features

- `simulate` (`simulate(params, steps, dt)`): Euler-integrate logistic growth; return [(t, value)] rows.
- `print_trajectory` (`print_trajectory(rows, every)`): Print every Nth trajectory row plus the final state.
- `main` (`main()`): Parse args, simulate, and print the trajectory and summary.
