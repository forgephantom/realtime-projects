# Sigma-Delta Noise Shaper: Simulation (Logistic Growth)

**Domain:** electronics_engineering | **Subdomain:** electronics_engineering | **Difficulty:** intermediate | **Est. build time:** 4-8 hours

---

## Overview

Sigma-Delta Noise Shaper built as a Simulation (logistic growth). A sigma-delta modulator oversamples and pushes quantization noise to high frequencies with a feedback loop, so a simple low-pass then yields high resolution from a 1-bit core. Euler-integrates logistic growth dP/dt = r*P*(1-P/K) and prints the trajectory table with summary statistics.

**Core concept — Sigma-Delta Noise Shaper:** A sigma-delta modulator oversamples and pushes quantization noise to high frequencies with a feedback loop, so a simple low-pass then yields high resolution from a 1-bit core.

**Why this matters in the real world:** Used in audio and precision sensor ADCs where high resolution matters more than conversion speed.

---

## Features

- `simulate` (`simulate(params, steps, dt)`): Euler-integrate logistic growth; return [(t, value)] rows.
- `print_trajectory` (`print_trajectory(rows, every)`): Print every Nth trajectory row plus the final state.
- `main` (`main()`): Parse args, simulate, and print the trajectory and summary.
