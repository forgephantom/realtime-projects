# Species Distribution Modeler: Naive Bayes Classifier

**Domain:** environmental_science | **Subdomain:** environmental_science | **Difficulty:** beginner | **Est. build time:** 2-4 hours

---

## Overview

Species Distribution Modeler built as a Naive Bayes classifier. Species distribution models relate occurrence records to climate and habitat variables, then project where else the species could live. The modeler fits the climate envelope and maps suitable habitat under current and future climates. Trains a Gaussian Naive Bayes classifier on seeded synthetic features and reports priors, accuracy, and the confusion matrix.

**Core concept — Species Distribution Modeler:** Species distribution models relate occurrence records to climate and habitat variables, then project where else the species could live. The modeler fits the climate envelope and maps suitable habitat under current and future climates.

**Why this matters in the real world:** Biologists forecast range shifts to plan protected areas under climate change.

---

## Features

- **GaussianNB**: Naive Bayes classifier with Gaussian feature likelihoods.
- `generate_dataset` (`generate_dataset(seed, n)`): Generate 2-feature topic vectors with binary labels.
- `split_dataset` (`split_dataset(X, y, train_frac, seed)`): Shuffle and split into (X_train, y_train, X_test, y_test).
- `GaussianNB.fit` (`GaussianNB.fit(self, X, y)`): Estimate per-class priors and per-feature (mean, var).
- `GaussianNB.predict` (`GaussianNB.predict(self, X)`): Return the most likely class for each feature vector.
- `main` (`main()`): Parse args, train, and print accuracy and confusion matrix.

## How It Works

The project is built around Gaussian Naive Bayes with log-space scoring. Input data flows from src/main.py through the processing modules, and the overall approach runs in O(n*d) to fit n samples with d features; O(c*d) per prediction. See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) for the high-level design and [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md) for the detailed module breakdown.
