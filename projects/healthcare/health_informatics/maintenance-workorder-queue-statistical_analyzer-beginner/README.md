# Facilities Work-Order Queue: Statistical Analyzer

**Domain:** healthcare | **Subdomain:** health_informatics | **Difficulty:** beginner | **Est. build time:** 2-4 hours

---

## Overview

Facilities Work-Order Queue built as a Statistical analyzer. A hospital facilities ticket system for synthetic work orders with priority, trade, location, and SLA targets. It ages open tickets, escalates SLA breaches, and reports completion rates by trade. Technicians are fictional staff. Draws a seeded synthetic sample and reports count, mean, median, stdev, quartiles, and an ASCII histogram.

**Core concept — Facilities Work-Order Queue:** A hospital facilities ticket system for synthetic work orders with priority, trade, location, and SLA targets. It ages open tickets, escalates SLA breaches, and reports completion rates by trade. Technicians are fictional staff.

**Why this matters in the real world:** Used by facilities management training on work-order triage.

---

## Features

- `generate_sample` (`generate_sample(seed, n)`): Draw synthetic topic measurements (mixture model).
- `percentile` (`percentile(sorted_vals, q)`): Linear-interpolation percentile of a pre-sorted list.
- `summarize` (`summarize(values, bins)`): Compute a full descriptive-statistics summary.
- `print_report` (`print_report(summary)`): Print the summary table and ASCII histogram.
- `main` (`main()`): Parse args and print the report.

## How It Works

The project is built around Descriptive statistics with linear-interpolation percentiles. Input data flows from src/main.py through the processing modules, and the overall approach runs in O(n log n) for the sort; O(n) for the histogram pass. See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) for the high-level design and [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md) for the detailed module breakdown.
